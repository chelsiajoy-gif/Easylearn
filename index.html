Meet the characters

Three pieces. One simple story.

No jargon test needed. Think of each number as a character in the ride.

🛟

Risk-free rate = the dock

Your starting point before you get on the roller coaster. It represents a low-risk return.

🎡

Market premium = the ride’s reward

The market’s expected return minus the dock’s rate. It is the extra reward for taking market risk.

🛒

Beta = your cart’s sensitivity

β = 1 moves roughly with the market; below 1 tends to swing less; above 1 tends to swing more.

🧭
Keep your feet on the ground: CAPM is a simplified estimate, not a guaranteed return or a stock-picking rule. Beta describes sensitivity to market moves; it does not capture every kind of risk.
